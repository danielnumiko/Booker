Home Games

Written by Ben Markovits
Recommended reading age: 8–12
ShortlistedThe Children's Booker Prize 2026
Published by Faber & Faber7 March 2026
Buy the book Buying books using the 'Buy the book' links helps support our charitable work.

Shortlisted for the Children's Booker Prize 2026, Home Games is a warm and funny story for younger readers from the acclaimed novelist Ben Markovits.

It's a book about family, friendship and finding your place — told with the eye for character and the quiet humour that run through all of Markovits's writing.

A big-hearted read for children aged 8 to 12, Home Games is a story about growing up, fitting in, and the games that hold a family together.

Ben Markovits Ben Markovits grew up in Texas, London and Berlin, and now lives in London. He is the author of several acclaimed novels and was named one of Granta's Best of Young British Novelists. More about Ben Markovits
