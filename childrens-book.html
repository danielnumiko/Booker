The Murderer's Ape

By Jakob Wegelius
Translated by Peter Graves
Published by Pushkin Children's Books
Original language: Swedish
Recommended reading age: 9–12
Recommended reading age: 9–12

Sally Jones is no ordinary ape. A gifted engineer and the loyal friend of the Chief, she works the great rivers and harbours of the world — until the Chief is accused of murder. To clear his name, Sally Jones follows a trail that winds from the docks of Lisbon to a maharaja's palace, in a mystery told entirely from her point of view.
